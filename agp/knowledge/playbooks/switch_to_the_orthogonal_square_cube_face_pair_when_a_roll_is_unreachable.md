# playbook: switch to the orthogonal square-cube face pair when a roll is unreachable

<!-- from session 20260904_124349_pyramid8, merged 2026-09-04 -->
1. Withdraw to clear air with the gripper open before changing wrist yaw.
2. Because a square cube has two equivalent perpendicular face pairs, subtract or add 90 degrees from the unreachable face-aligned roll.
3. If the yaw change is large, split it into intermediate high-air orientations and verify each motion completed.
4. Approach the isolated source with the orthogonal roll, center at the top face, descend in stages, and require a nominal face-width close plus a short-lift wrist-image hold check.
5. Keep that roll during transport. At placement, account for the altered finger-opening direction and use only a partial release before lifting vertically.
