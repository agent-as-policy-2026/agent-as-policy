# playbook: cumulative in-air yaw correction by verified increments

<!-- from session 20260903_182646_pyramid6, merged 2026-09-03 -->
1. Start only from a centered, face-aligned grasp that has survived a short verification lift.
2. Lift the cube to about `z=0.10 m` in clear air and re-close; require the demonstrated centered width of about `0.0495 m`.
3. Apply one 15-degree base-z yaw increment with no translation.
4. Capture the wrist image. Continue only if the cube remains large and fixed between the pads.
5. Re-close and repeat steps 3-4 until the required yaw is reached. This run demonstrated three consecutive increments (about 45 degrees total) on two cubes.
6. Preserve the resulting yaw during segmented transport; re-close and verify between segments.
