## tool: geometry.py

Converts supplied wrist pixels/depth to base XYZ or projects supplied camera pixels onto specified z planes, consolidating the point-sampling and ray-plane helpers.

## tool: measure.py

Computes XYZ percentiles and a visible-extrema midpoint from a supplied ROI with optional RGB/height bands, consolidating ring and white-post measurements without claiming a circle fit.
