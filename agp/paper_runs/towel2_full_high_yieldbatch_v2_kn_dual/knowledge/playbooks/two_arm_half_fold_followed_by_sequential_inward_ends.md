1. Study the demonstration, obtain both states and wrist/overhead captures, and measure current corners. All x/y values below are examples dependent on towel placement.
2. Approach each near corner with its own arm, downward q=(0,1,0,0), grasping the side border about 20–30 mm from the near end. Successful commanded examples were left (0.130,0.001,-0.033) and right (0.151,-0.714,-0.034). Use the measured local table height; inspect achieved height.
3. Close and capture. Lift approximately 60 mm and inspect both wrist images. In this run the initial right grasp failed; an inward and lower replacement grasp succeeded. Do not infer a hold from a ~0.01 residual fraction alone.
4. Carry both corners concurrently, maintaining lateral separation, through example apex positions left (0.310,0.001,0.150), right (0.331,-0.715,0.150), q=(0,0.965926,0,0.258819). Observe.
5. Continue through left (0.475,0.001,0.090), right (0.496,-0.707,0.090), q=(0,0.92388,0,0.382683). Observe achieved poses and cloth.
6. Lower near the corresponding far corners at z=-0.022 m with the same tilt; this run used x=0.525/0.545 and then a 25 mm forward adjustment. Release, observe, retreat approximately 100 mm backward and upward, and observe. A narrow curled free-edge strip remained.
7. Re-measure the doubled short edges. Left midpoint grasp example (0.460,0.032,-0.033), downward; verify close and lift. Carry with 45-degree forward tilt through (0.460,-0.140,0.110), then lower to (0.467,-0.315,-0.016). Release, observe, withdraw, then home left.
8. Right midpoint grasp example (0.473,-0.686,-0.034), downward; verify close and lift. Carry with 45-degree forward tilt through (0.473,-0.515,0.100), then lower to (0.462,-0.350,-0.016). Reassess the seam because the body can slide. This run's additional 60 mm inward correction mostly translated the whole towel.
9. Release and observe; retreat approximately 120 mm backward/upward and then home right after checking left state and clearance. Final overhead capture must show both inward flaps and the half-fold, open grippers, and withdrawn arms. The demonstrated outcome had modest offsets and curled corners.
