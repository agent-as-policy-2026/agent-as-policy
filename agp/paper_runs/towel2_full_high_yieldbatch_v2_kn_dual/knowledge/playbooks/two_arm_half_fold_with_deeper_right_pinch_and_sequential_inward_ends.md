This procedure completed the intended folds with a skewed right flap and a curled free edge. All x/y coordinates below are placement-dependent examples; re-measure them. Heights also require checking the local table and achieved pose.

1. Study the demonstration; capture both states and wrist views plus overhead. Measure the flat towel corners and local table heights.
2. Approach each near corner on its own side with downward quaternion (0,1,0,0). Left successful example: (0.126,-0.010,-0.034), about 25 mm behind the near edge. Close, capture, lift 60 mm, capture again.
3. For the right side, use a substantial inward bite rather than the very outer hem. Successful recovery example after towel displacement: (0.125,-0.651,-0.0345), achieved z=-0.0319, 8.5 mm opening. An 80 mm lift visibly raised the cloth. Earlier 7.0 mm grip slipped, so inspect actual cloth support after every segment.
4. Carry both sides concurrently while keeping at least about 0.65 m lateral gripper separation. Example apex left (0.310,-0.010,0.150), right (0.300,-0.665,0.150), quaternion (0,0.965926,0,0.258819). Inspect.
5. Continue through left (0.460,-0.010,0.075), right (0.445,-0.675,0.075), quaternion (0,0.92388,0,0.382683). Inspect. Lower to the corresponding far-edge region, examples left (0.525,-0.010,-0.020), right (0.492,-0.685,-0.020). Release and retreat 100 mm backward, 120 mm upward. A free-edge curl remained.
6. Withdraw right. Re-measure left doubled short edge. Pinch its midpoint downward, example (0.450,0.002,-0.033); close, inspect, lift 65 mm, inspect layers.
7. Carry left with 45-degree forward tilt through (0.450,-0.160,0.105) to (0.450,-0.335,-0.014), release, retreat backward/upward and toward its own side, then home. Re-observe the shifted towel.
8. Pinch current right doubled short edge downward, example (0.430,-0.665,-0.032); close and lift 60 mm with visual checks. Carry with 45-degree forward tilt through (0.430,-0.490,0.100) to (0.428,-0.320,-0.013). Release and inspect. Expect possible body translation; this run left a skewed flap.
9. Retreat right 110 mm backward, 80 mm toward its side, 120 mm upward, then home. Confirm both open states and the unobstructed final overhead view.
