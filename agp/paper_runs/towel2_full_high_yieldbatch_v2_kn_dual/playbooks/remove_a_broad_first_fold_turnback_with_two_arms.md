# playbook: remove a broad first-fold turnback with two arms

<!-- from session 20260909_110329_paper_towel_full_high_yieldbatch_v2_kn_dual_03, merged 2026-09-09 -->
This is a verified subprocedure for removing the broad raised band; it is not a verified complete folding solution. All x/y coordinates and contact heights below are placement-dependent examples from this run. Re-perceive the band, table, and both arms before using them.

1. Observe both ends of the raised band with the wrist cameras. In this run the left raised cloth was near (0.519,-0.003,-0.010) m and the right near (0.479,-0.683,-0.022) m.
2. Open each gripper to fraction 0.35 (about 33 mm). Use q=(0.270598,0.653281,0.653281,0.270598), which points the tool forward/down with jaws closing roughly along x.
3. Approach the raised band at left (0.520,-0.002,0.004) m and right (0.477,-0.683,-0.011) m. These centers are above the measured cloth; tilted finger geometry must remain above the table.
4. Close, inspect the returned width or stall state, and lift 35 mm. The right reported a stall and approximately 8.4 mm width; the left thin hold required visual verification. Verify both ends move with the jaws before carrying.
5. Extend forward to left (0.595,-0.003,0.005) m and right (0.560,-0.682,0.005) m with q=(0.353553,0.612372,0.612372,0.353553), a greater forward tilt. Keep the arms on their separated sides.
6. Release and retreat, then inspect overhead. This removed the broad band in `frames/0019_top.png`, though a layer offset remained. Do not infer perfect first-fold alignment from removal of the band alone.
